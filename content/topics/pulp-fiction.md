## Overview
*Pulp Fiction* interweaves criminals, a boxer, a crime boss, and a mysterious briefcase across several connected stories.
## Key Facts
- Directed by Quentin Tarantino
- Stars John Travolta, Samuel L. Jackson, and Uma Thurman
- Won the Palme d’Or
## Production and History
Produced independently and distributed by Miramax, the film became an unexpected commercial and cultural success.
## Story Structure and Style
Events appear out of chronological order, changing how consequences and character choices are understood. Long conversations, sudden violence, pop references, and found music create its rhythm.
## Impact / Legacy
The film energized American independent cinema and influenced dialogue, editing, soundtracks, and crime storytelling.
## Did You Know?
- The glowing briefcase’s contents are never explained.
- John Travolta’s casting revived his career.
- Tarantino and Roger Avary won the screenplay Oscar.

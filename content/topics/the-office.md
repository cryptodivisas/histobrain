## Overview

*The Office* is an American workplace sitcom developed by Greg Daniels from the British series created by Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant. Set at the Scranton branch of the fictional Dunder Mifflin paper company, it follows manager Michael Scott and employees whose ordinary workdays become awkward, competitive, and unexpectedly affectionate.

## Key Facts

- Aired for nine seasons on NBC
- Stars Steve Carell, Rainn Wilson, John Krasinski, Jenna Fischer, and an ensemble cast
- Uses a documentary crew as part of its fictional premise
- Adapted from the British series of the same name

## History and Development

The early American episodes closely resembled the British original, but the series soon developed a warmer tone and expanded its supporting cast. Michael changed from a mostly harsh boss into a deeply insecure person who wants friendship as much as authority. The romance between Jim and Pam provided a serialized emotional center alongside workplace stories.

## Format and Structure

The mockumentary format uses handheld cameras, zooms, glances toward the lens, and private interview segments. Characters can contradict themselves within seconds as the camera observes both their claim and the reality. Cold opens often present self-contained office absurdities, while episodes interweave several workplace plots.

## Impact / Legacy

The show helped popularize single-camera mockumentary comedy on American television and influenced series such as *Parks and Recreation* and *Modern Family*. Streaming introduced it to new audiences, turning background characters, brief reactions, and individual scenes into widely shared online references.

## Did You Know?

- Several cast members also wrote or directed episodes.
- The opening footage of Scranton was filmed by John Krasinski.
- Michael’s improvised meetings often reveal more about him than the topic being discussed.
- The documentary crew becomes more directly acknowledged during the final season.

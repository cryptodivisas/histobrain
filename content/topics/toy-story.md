## Overview
Woody fears replacement when Buzz Lightyear becomes Andy’s favorite new toy, forcing the rivals into an adventure together.
## Key Facts
- First fully computer-animated feature film
- Directed by John Lasseter
- Produced by Pixar and released by Disney
## Production and History
Pixar developed new tools for feature-length digital character animation while repeatedly revising the story and personalities.
## Story Structure and Style
The buddy-comedy structure turns rivalry into cooperation. Camera height, texture, and scale present ordinary rooms as enormous environments from a toy’s viewpoint.
## Impact / Legacy
The film launched Pixar’s feature business and accelerated the animation industry’s move toward CGI.
## Did You Know?
- Tom Hanks voices Woody.
- Tim Allen voices Buzz.
- Randy Newman wrote “You’ve Got a Friend in Me.”

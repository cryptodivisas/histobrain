## Overview
Frank Darabont’s film follows banker Andy Dufresne through decades in Shawshank prison and his friendship with fellow inmate Red.
## Key Facts
- Stars Tim Robbins and Morgan Freeman
- Adapted from a Stephen King novella
- Narrated by Red
## Production and History
The film received strong reviews but modest theatrical attendance. Television, rental, and home-video exposure steadily expanded its audience.
## Story Structure and Style
Red’s narration turns prison routines into a long memory. Repeated locations and time jumps show institutionalization, while Andy’s patient plans give small details later meaning.
## Impact / Legacy
Its humane performances and emphasis on hope made it one of modern cinema’s most beloved dramas.
## Did You Know?
- The prison exterior was filmed at Ohio State Reformatory.
- King’s novella has a longer title.
- The film received seven Oscar nominations.

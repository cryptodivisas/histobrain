title: Back to the Future
era: 1985
summary: Time-travel comedy in which Marty McFly must repair his parents’ history before returning to 1985.
---
## Overview
Marty accidentally travels from 1985 to 1955 in Doc Brown’s DeLorean and disrupts the meeting that created his family.
## Key Facts
- Directed by Robert Zemeckis
- Stars Michael J. Fox and Christopher Lloyd
- Produced by Steven Spielberg’s Amblin Entertainment
## Production and History
The screenplay was initially rejected repeatedly. Michael J. Fox joined after filming had begun with another actor, requiring major reshoots.
## Story Structure and Style
Mirrored versions of Hill Valley turn small background details into setup and payoff. A clock-tower deadline joins Marty’s family problem to the mechanics of returning home.
## Impact / Legacy
The film became a model of tightly constructed popular storytelling and launched two sequels.
## Did You Know?
- The time machine was originally imagined differently.
- Huey Lewis appears briefly as a judge.
- The clock stops at 10:04.

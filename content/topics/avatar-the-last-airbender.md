## Overview
Aang, the last Air Nomad and current Avatar, travels with friends to stop the Fire Nation’s century-long war.
## Key Facts
- Created by Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko
- Organized as Water, Earth, and Fire “books”
- Blends comedy, action, and serialized drama
## History and Development
The creators combined Asian and Indigenous cultural influences, martial-arts research, anime, and Western television storytelling.
## Format and Structure
Each bending style draws from a different martial art. Episodic destinations steadily advance a planned three-season arc, while Zuko’s pursuit develops into a redemption story.
## Impact / Legacy
The series earned lasting acclaim for worldbuilding, character growth, and moral complexity in children’s animation.
## Did You Know?
- Aang’s arrows glow in the Avatar State.
- Each season corresponds to an element.
- The original animated series has no filler season beyond its central war.

---
title: The X-Files
era: 1993–2018
summary: Science-fiction drama pairing believer Fox Mulder and skeptic Dana Scully in paranormal investigations.
---
## Overview
FBI agents Mulder and Scully investigate unexplained cases while uncovering a larger government conspiracy.
## Key Facts
- Created by Chris Carter
- Stars David Duchovny and Gillian Anderson
- Popularized “The truth is out there”
## History and Development
The series grew from a cult genre program into a mainstream 1990s hit and later returned for revival seasons.
## Format and Structure
Standalone “monster-of-the-week” episodes alternate with continuing mythology. Mulder and Scully’s opposing methods create debate, but experience gradually complicates both positions.
## Impact / Legacy
It influenced serialized mystery, online fandom, and the visual language of paranormal television.
## Did You Know?
- Early production was based in Vancouver.
- The Cigarette Smoking Man began as a nearly silent figure.
- Mark Snow composed the whistling theme.

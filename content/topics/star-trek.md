title: Star Trek
era: 1966–1969
summary: Optimistic science-fiction series following the diverse USS Enterprise crew on a mission of exploration.
---
## Overview
Captain Kirk, Spock, Dr. McCoy, and the Enterprise crew explore unfamiliar worlds and confront ethical conflicts.
## Key Facts
- Created by Gene Roddenberry
- Stars William Shatner and Leonard Nimoy
- Originally ran for three seasons
## History and Development
The show struggled in network ratings but developed a committed audience. Syndication after cancellation allowed new viewers to discover it and supported films and later series.
## Format and Structure
Planet-of-the-week adventures combine exploration with debates among Kirk’s instinct, Spock’s logic, and McCoy’s humanism.
## Impact / Legacy
Its cooperative future, diverse crew, and fan culture created one of entertainment’s largest franchises.
## Did You Know?
- A letter campaign helped secure a third season.
- Nichelle Nichols later helped NASA recruit astronauts.
- The Vulcan salute came from Leonard Nimoy’s childhood memories.

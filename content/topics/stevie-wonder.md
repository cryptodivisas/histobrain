## Overview
Stevie Wonder is an American singer, songwriter, producer, and multi-instrumentalist who progressed from child prodigy to one of popular music’s most accomplished album artists.
## Key Facts
- Signed with Motown as a child
- Created *Innervisions* and *Songs in the Key of Life*
- Known for “Superstition” and “Sir Duke”
## History and Career
Initially promoted as Little Stevie Wonder, he achieved an early hit with “Fingertips.” As an adult he negotiated greater creative control and produced a remarkable 1970s run of albums addressing love, spirituality, inequality, and urban life.
## Musical Style and Structure
Wonder layers keyboards, harmonica, drums, bass, and vocals into elastic grooves. Synthesizers became expressive lead instruments, while unexpected chord changes support highly singable melodies.
## Impact / Legacy
His work expanded the artistic authority of soul musicians and influenced R&B, pop, jazz, hip-hop, and electronic production.
## Did You Know?
- Wonder has been blind since infancy.
- He played many instruments on his classic albums.
- His advocacy helped establish Martin Luther King Jr. Day as a U.S. federal holiday.

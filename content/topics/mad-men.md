## Overview
*Mad Men* uses advertising careers and private lives to examine identity, ambition, gender, work, and consumer culture.
## Key Facts
- Created by Matthew Weiner
- Stars Jon Hamm and Elisabeth Moss
- Runs for seven seasons
## History and Development
Weiner wrote the pilot before joining *The Sopranos*. AMC’s production became a defining early success for the network’s original dramas.
## Format and Structure
Client campaigns mirror personal conflicts without reducing them to simple lessons. Time jumps between seasons let fashion, politics, and workplace expectations change around the characters.
## Impact / Legacy
The show influenced period design, slow-burn character drama, and television’s treatment of work.
## Did You Know?
- The agency changes names several times.
- Real advertisements are woven into fictional pitches.
- “Carousel” is one of its most celebrated presentations.

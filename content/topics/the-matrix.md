## Overview
Computer hacker Neo learns that everyday reality is a simulation and joins Morpheus and Trinity’s resistance.
## Key Facts
- Written and directed by the Wachowskis
- Stars Keanu Reeves, Laurence Fishburne, and Carrie-Anne Moss
- Introduced “bullet time” to mass audiences
## Production and History
The filmmakers combined Hong Kong action choreography, cyberpunk fiction, anime, philosophy, and digital-effects research.
## Story Structure and Style
Neo’s awakening follows a chosen-hero arc while repeatedly questioning perception. Green-tinted simulation scenes, wire-assisted combat, and rotating camera arrays create a distinct visual language.
## Impact / Legacy
The film reshaped action design and made its red-pill choice, code imagery, and costumes cultural shorthand.
## Did You Know?
- Cast members trained for months in martial arts.
- The lobby sequence mixed practical destruction with wire work.
- The film won four Academy Awards.

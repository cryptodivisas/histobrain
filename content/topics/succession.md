title: Succession

## Overview
As Logan Roy’s health and plans remain uncertain, his children repeatedly maneuver to inherit Waystar Royco.
## Key Facts
- Created by Jesse Armstrong
- Stars Brian Cox, Jeremy Strong, Sarah Snook, and Kieran Culkin
- Runs for four seasons
## History and Development
Armstrong drew from research into media dynasties and earlier political satire. Improvisation and overlapping dialogue helped give corporate scenes restless energy.
## Format and Structure
Board votes, acquisitions, elections, and retreats become family battlegrounds. Handheld cameras search faces for reactions, while insults and jokes conceal emotional dependence.
## Impact / Legacy
The series became a defining satire of inherited wealth, media power, and damaged family systems.
## Did You Know?
- The theme was composed by Nicholas Britell.
- Scripts include deliberately extravagant insults.
- The title sequence mixes invented family footage with corporate imagery.

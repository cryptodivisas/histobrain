title: The Dark Knight
era: 2008
summary: Christopher Nolan’s Batman crime thriller about escalation, civic order, sacrifice, and the Joker’s campaign of chaos.
---
## Overview
Batman, Jim Gordon, and prosecutor Harvey Dent challenge organized crime until the Joker turns Gotham’s people and institutions against one another.
## Key Facts
- Directed by Christopher Nolan
- Stars Christian Bale and Heath Ledger
- Features large-format IMAX photography
## Production and History
Nolan approached the sequel as an urban crime epic, filming major sequences practically in Chicago and other cities.
## Story Structure and Style
Interlocking moral traps replace a simple pursuit plot. Crosscut deadlines, practical stunts, surveillance imagery, and Hans Zimmer and James Newton Howard’s tense score sustain pressure.
## Impact / Legacy
The film broadened expectations for superhero drama and influenced the Academy’s later Best Picture expansion.
## Did You Know?
- Ledger won a posthumous supporting-actor Oscar.
- A full-size truck was flipped on a city street.
- Several sequences were shot with IMAX cameras.

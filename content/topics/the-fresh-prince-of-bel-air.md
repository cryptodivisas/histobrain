---
title: The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air
era: 1990–1996
summary: Family sitcom in which Will Smith’s Philadelphia teenager adjusts to life with wealthy relatives in Bel-Air.
---
## Overview
Will’s energy and streetwise confidence collide with the formal Banks household, but both sides reshape one another.
## Key Facts
- Stars Will Smith
- Runs for six seasons
- Theme song summarizes the premise
## History and Development
Producer Quincy Jones helped build the show around rapper Will Smith, giving him an acting breakthrough and a fictionalized version of his persona.
## Format and Structure
Fish-out-of-water comedy drives the premise, while family stories address class, race, responsibility, identity, and absent parents.
## Impact / Legacy
The series became a durable global sitcom and launched Smith’s screen career.
## Did You Know?
- Two actors played Aunt Vivian.
- Alfonso Ribeiro created Carlton’s famous dance.
- The Bel-Air mansion exterior is actually in Brentwood.

## Overview

*Jurassic Park* is a 1993 science-fiction adventure directed by Steven Spielberg and based on Michael Crichton’s novel. On a remote island, entrepreneur John Hammond builds a theme park populated by dinosaurs cloned from ancient DNA. A systems failure turns a guided inspection into a struggle for survival and exposes the danger of treating living systems as controllable products.

## Key Facts

- Directed by Steven Spielberg
- Based on Michael Crichton’s 1990 novel
- Stars Sam Neill, Laura Dern, Jeff Goldblum, and Richard Attenborough
- Effects created through Stan Winston’s animatronics and Industrial Light & Magic’s CGI

## Production and History

The filmmakers initially expected to use stop-motion for many full-body dinosaurs. Tests by Industrial Light & Magic demonstrated that computer-generated creatures could move convincingly, while Stan Winston’s team built life-sized animatronic dinosaurs for close interaction. The combination gave the creatures weight, texture, and range rather than relying on one technique.

## Story Structure and Style

The film begins with wonder: the first full dinosaur reveal is staged through the characters’ astonished reactions. It gradually becomes a suspense story as fences fail and separate groups cross the park. Spielberg often delays a creature’s appearance through sound, rippling water, shadows, or moving objects. Scientific explanations are simplified through the animated Mr. DNA presentation.

## Impact / Legacy

*Jurassic Park* proved that digital characters could share live-action space convincingly and accelerated the film industry’s adoption of CGI. It launched a major franchise and shaped public imagination about dinosaurs, even as later paleontology revised details such as skin and feathers.

## Did You Know?

- The Tyrannosaurus rex animatronic could weigh several tons.
- A glass of water was used to signal the approaching T. rex.
- The velociraptors were enlarged and altered for dramatic effect.
- Spielberg supervised post-production while preparing *Schindler’s List*.

## Overview

*Parasite* is a 2019 South Korean film directed by Bong Joon Ho. The struggling Kim family gradually secures jobs in the wealthy Park household by posing as unrelated specialists. What begins as a dark social comedy becomes a thriller as secrets inside the Park home expose deeper layers of inequality and desperation.

## Key Facts

- Directed by Bong Joon Ho
- Written by Bong Joon Ho and Han Jin Won
- Stars Song Kang Ho, Lee Sun Kyun, Cho Yeo Jeong, and Choi Woo Shik
- First non-English-language film to win the Academy Award for Best Picture

## Production and History

Bong drew partly on his experience tutoring a wealthy family when he was young. The Park house and surrounding property were carefully designed as sets so that sightlines, staircases, windows, and levels could support the story. Production designer Lee Ha Jun created a home that feels architecturally real while functioning as a precise cinematic machine.

## Story Structure and Style

The film repeatedly uses vertical movement to represent social position. The Parks live high in a modern house; the Kims occupy a semi-basement; other characters descend still farther. Windows, smells, rain, and stairs recur as motifs. Tonal shifts from comedy to suspense and tragedy happen without abandoning the same central conflict over access and dignity.

## Impact / Legacy

*Parasite* won the Palme d’Or at Cannes and four Academy Awards, including Best Picture and Best Director. Its success widened international attention to Korean cinema and demonstrated that a subtitled social thriller could become a global mainstream event.

## Did You Know?

- The Park house was built as interconnected sets rather than filmed in an existing home.
- The invented noodle dish “ram-don” combines two inexpensive instant noodle brands with expensive beef.
- Rain brings relief to one family while devastating another.
- Bong Joon Ho described the film as a comedy without clowns and a tragedy without villains.

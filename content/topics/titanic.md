## Overview

*Titanic* is a 1997 epic romance and disaster film written and directed by James Cameron. Leonardo DiCaprio and Kate Winslet play Jack and Rose, fictional passengers from different social classes who meet aboard the RMS Titanic. Their story unfolds against the documented 1912 sinking, allowing the film to connect personal freedom, class division, and historical tragedy.

## Key Facts

- Written and directed by James Cameron
- Stars Kate Winslet, Leonardo DiCaprio, Billy Zane, and Gloria Stuart
- Music composed by James Horner
- Won 11 Academy Awards, including Best Picture

## Production and History

Cameron’s fascination with shipwrecks led him to film the real Titanic wreck before dramatizing the voyage. A near-full-scale exterior set was constructed in Mexico, while detailed interiors recreated the ship from plans and historical research. The production became famous for its cost, technical difficulty, water work, miniatures, and emerging digital effects.

## Story Structure and Style

An elderly Rose narrates the central story from the present, framing the past as memory. The first half builds the shipboard world and the romance; the collision marks a shift into a carefully timed disaster narrative. Class barriers shape both the relationship and the evacuation, while recurring objects such as the drawing and necklace connect the timelines.

## Impact / Legacy

*Titanic* became a global phenomenon and one of the highest-grossing films ever released. It renewed public interest in the historical ship and demonstrated that effects-heavy spectacle could be driven by romance and character. Its visual recreation of the sinking became the popular reference point for the event.

## Did You Know?

- Cameron made multiple dives to the Titanic wreck.
- Gloria Stuart became one of the oldest acting Oscar nominees for playing older Rose.
- The musicians continuing to play during the sinking were based on historical accounts.
- “My Heart Will Go On” was performed by Celine Dion.

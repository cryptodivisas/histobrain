## Overview
Nirvana was an American rock band formed by Kurt Cobain and Krist Novoselic, with Dave Grohl joining as its best-known drummer. The group became the defining popular breakthrough of Seattle’s grunge scene.
## Key Facts
- Released *Nevermind* in 1991
- Known for “Smells Like Teen Spirit”
- Combined punk abrasion with pop melody
## History and Career
After the independent album *Bleach*, Nirvana signed with DGC Records. *Nevermind* unexpectedly became a mass success and displaced polished hard rock from the center of youth culture. *In Utero* answered fame with a harsher sound. The band ended after Cobain’s death in 1994.
## Musical Style and Structure
Nirvana often alternated quiet verses with explosive choruses. Distorted power chords, direct drumming, and Cobain’s strained voice surrounded melodies that remained memorable beneath the noise.
## Impact / Legacy
The band brought alternative rock to a huge audience and reshaped fashion, radio, and the music industry.
## Did You Know?
- The swimming baby on *Nevermind* became an iconic album cover.
- Dave Grohl later founded Foo Fighters.
- Nirvana’s acoustic MTV performance was released as a celebrated live album.

## Overview
*The Wire* begins with a police investigation but expands each season into another part of Baltimore’s civic system.
## Key Facts
- Created by former reporter David Simon
- Ran for five seasons on HBO
- Uses a large ensemble cast
## History and Development
Simon and former detective Ed Burns drew on reporting, policing, teaching, and city politics. Many local performers and public figures appeared.
## Format and Structure
Patient serialization follows organizations rather than a single hero. Parallel chains of command reveal similar incentives across police, drug crews, ports, government, schools, and media.
## Impact / Legacy
Its institutional perspective became a benchmark for socially ambitious television drama.
## Did You Know?
- Each season emphasizes a different institution.
- Baltimore is treated almost as a central character.
- Several actors had direct experience with the worlds portrayed.

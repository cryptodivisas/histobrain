---
title: I Love Lucy
era: 1951–1957
summary: Landmark sitcom built around Lucille Ball’s physical comedy and Lucy Ricardo’s determination to enter show business.
---
## Overview
Lucy Ricardo’s elaborate plans repeatedly disrupt domestic life with her bandleader husband Ricky and friends Fred and Ethel Mertz.
## Key Facts
- Stars Lucille Ball and Desi Arnaz
- Produced by Desilu
- Used a three-camera setup before an audience
## History and Development
Ball and Arnaz formed Desilu and insisted they play the married leads. Filming on 35mm created unusually durable, high-quality episodes.
## Format and Structure
Clear goals escalate through misunderstandings and physical complications before a comic collapse. Multiple cameras preserve performance energy while providing editable coverage.
## Impact / Legacy
The series shaped reruns, sitcom production, and television comedy.
## Did You Know?
- Desilu later produced *Star Trek*.
- The chocolate-factory scene became iconic.
- Ball’s pregnancy was written into the series.

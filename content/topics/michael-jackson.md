## Overview

Michael Jackson was an American singer, songwriter, dancer, and entertainer whose career spanned childhood stardom and unprecedented solo success. Known as the “King of Pop,” he combined precise vocals, rhythmic movement, ambitious short films, and large-scale stage production. *Thriller* became one of the best-selling albums in recorded-music history.

## Key Facts

- Began performing professionally with the Jackson 5
- Major solo albums include *Off the Wall*, *Thriller*, *Bad*, and *Dangerous*
- Worked closely with producer Quincy Jones on three landmark albums
- Popularized signature movements including the moonwalk

## History and Career

Jackson and his brothers achieved Motown success with songs such as “I Want You Back.” As an adult solo artist, he moved beyond the group’s sound with *Off the Wall* in 1979. *Thriller* followed in 1982 and generated a remarkable sequence of hit singles. Later tours and albums reinforced his international fame, although his private life, changing appearance, and legal controversies received intense media scrutiny.

## Performance Style and Sound

Jackson’s recordings blend pop, soul, funk, rock, and dance music. His vocal style includes rhythmic breaths, sharp exclamations, layered harmonies, and dramatic shifts between tenderness and intensity. Choreography was inseparable from the songs: tightly controlled isolations, spins, and group routines made televised performances and music videos major events.

## Impact / Legacy

Jackson helped make music videos central to pop promotion and broke important racial barriers on music television. The films for “Thriller,” “Beat It,” and “Billie Jean” influenced generations of performers. His singing, dancing, fashion, and concert spectacle remain reference points in global pop culture.

## Did You Know?

- “Thriller” was directed by filmmaker John Landis.
- Jackson debuted the moonwalk on a 1983 television special.
- Eddie Van Halen played the guitar solo on “Beat It.”
- Jackson won a record-setting eight Grammy Awards in one night in 1984.
